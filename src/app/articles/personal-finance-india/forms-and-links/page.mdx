# 18. Forms, Schemes and Official Links

*Every portal, form and scheme referenced in this document, with the official source for each. Verified August 2026.*

> **Use official portals only.** Every service listed here is free or fixed-fee at the source. Intermediaries charging to "activate your UAN", "file your EPF claim" or "improve your CIBIL score" are selling you something you can do yourself in minutes. Government URLs occasionally move; if a link fails, search for the institution's name plus `gov.in` rather than clicking a sponsored result.

## 18.1 Income tax

| What | Where |
|---|---|
| **File your return, view AIS/TIS, e-verify, link PAN–Aadhaar, respond to notices** | [incometax.gov.in](https://www.incometax.gov.in), the e-filing portal |
| Acts, rules, circulars, notifications, the Income-tax Act 2025 text | [incometaxindia.gov.in](https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in) |
| **Form 168** (formerly 26AS): consolidated tax statement | Via the e-filing portal, or [TRACES](https://www.tdscpc.gov.in) |
| Annual Information Statement (AIS) and Taxpayer Information Summary (TIS) | e-filing portal → *Services → AIS* |
| Apply for or correct **PAN** | [Protean (NSDL) TIN](https://www.protean-tinpan.com) or [UTIITSL](https://www.pan.utiitsl.com) |
| Pay advance tax / self-assessment tax | e-filing portal → *e-Pay Tax* |
| Check refund status | e-filing portal → *Services → Know Your Refund Status* |

### Tax forms you will actually encounter

| Form | Formerly | What it is | Who gives it to you |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Form 130** | Form 16 | Salary TDS certificate | Employer, by ~June |
| **Form 131** | Form 16A | TDS certificate, non-salary | Bank / payer |
| **Form 124** | Form 12BB | Investment & rent declaration to your employer | You → employer, Jan–Feb |
| **Form 168** | Form 26AS | Consolidated tax statement | e-filing portal / TRACES |
| **Form 121** | Forms 15G / 15H | Declaration for non-deduction of TDS | You → bank or EPFO |
| Form 67 | n/a | Claim foreign tax credit | You, before filing |
| Form 10-IEA *(successor form)* | n/a | Opt out of the new regime (business income only) | You, before filing |
| ITR-1 / ITR-2 / ITR-3 | n/a | Return forms. **RSU or foreign-share holders need ITR-2 or ITR-3**, never ITR-1 | e-filing portal |
| Schedule FA | n/a | Foreign assets disclosure, **no minimum value** | Inside ITR-2 / ITR-3 |
| Schedule VDA | n/a | Crypto and other Virtual Digital Assets | Inside your ITR |

**Deadlines:** ITR-1 and ITR-2 by **31 July**; ITR-3 and ITR-4 by **31 August**; revised return by **31 March**. See [§17.13](/articles/personal-finance-india/numbers#1713-compliance-calendar).

## 18.2 EPF, EPS and gratuity

| What | Where |
|---|---|
| **Member portal**: activate UAN, seed KYC, file claims, e-nomination, transfer | [unifiedportal-mem.epfindia.gov.in](https://unifiedportal-mem.epfindia.gov.in/memberinterface/) |
| **Passbook**: see employee share, employer share, EPS and interest | [passbook.epfindia.gov.in](https://passbook.epfindia.gov.in) |
| EPFO main site, circulars, downloadable forms | [epfindia.gov.in](https://www.epfindia.gov.in) |
| **File a grievance** | [epfigms.gov.in](https://epfigms.gov.in) |
| UMANG app | EPFO services on mobile, including passbook and claim status |

### EPF forms

| Form | What it does | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Form 11** | Declaration on joining a new employer | Records prior membership, UAN, KYC, "excluded employee" status. **It does not increase your contribution**; see [§7.5](/articles/personal-finance-india/epf-eps-form-11#75-form-11-what-it-actually-is) |
| **Para 26(6) joint declaration** | Contribute on wages **above** the ₹15,000 ceiling | Signed by **both** you and your employer, filed with the EPFO office, with Form 11 and a salary slip. Employer consent is required |
| **Form 2** / e-nomination | Nominate for EPF, EPS and EDLI | Do this online via the member portal |
| **Form 13** | Transfer PF from a previous employer | Largely automatic now where UAN and KYC are correct |
| **Form 19** | Final settlement of EPF | On retirement, or after two months of unemployment |
| **Form 10C** | Withdraw EPS corpus | Where eligible service is under 10 years |
| **Form 10D** | Claim monthly EPS pension | At 58, or early from 50 at a reduced rate |
| **Form 31** | Partial advance from EPF | Housing, marriage, education, medical, etc. See [§7.8](/articles/personal-finance-india/epf-eps-form-11#78-withdrawals-and-advances) |
| **Form 121** | Avoid TDS on an early withdrawal | Only if total income is below the taxable limit |

**Gratuity** is claimed from your employer, not from EPFO, using **Form I** under the Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972, within 30 days of it becoming payable.

## 18.4 Small savings: PPF, SSY, NSC, SCSS

| What | Where |
|---|---|
| Open and operate PPF, SSY, NSC, SCSS, POMIS, KVP | [India Post](https://www.indiapost.gov.in), or a designated branch of most public-sector and several private banks |
| Scheme rules, forms, current rates | [National Savings Institute](https://www.nsiindia.gov.in) |
| Quarterly rate notifications | Department of Economic Affairs, Ministry of Finance |

**Forms:** Form A to open an account, Form B to deposit, Form C to withdraw, Form D for a loan, Form H to extend a PPF account beyond 15 years. Most banks now support PPF and SSY entirely through net banking.

**Timing note:** PPF interest is computed on the lowest balance between the **5th and the last day** of each month. Deposit by the 5th.

| What | Where |
|---|---|
| Regulator: regulations, master circulars, insurer lists | [IRDAI](https://irdai.gov.in) |
| **File a complaint against an insurer** | [Bima Bharosa](https://bimabharosa.irdai.gov.in) |

| Check whether an agent or broker is registered | IRDAI portal → intermediary registers |

**Before buying:** confirm the room-rent limit, co-pay, sub-limits, PED waiting period and restoration terms; these determine whether a policy pays. See [§9.5](/articles/personal-finance-india/insurance#95-health-insurance).

**Escalation path:** insurer's grievance officer → Bima Bharosa → Insurance Ombudsman. Each stage has a defined response window.

## 18.7 Banking, credit and credit bureaus

| What | Where |
|---|---|
| Regulator: circulars, master directions, rates | [RBI](https://www.rbi.org.in) |
| **Complain about a bank, NBFC or card issuer** (RBI Ombudsman) | [RBI CMS](https://cms.rbi.org.in) |
| **Check whether an entity is authorised to take deposits or lend** | [RBI Sachet](https://sachet.rbi.org.in) |
| Deposit insurance: what is covered, and claims | [DICGC](https://www.dicgc.org.in) |

### The four credit bureaus

You are entitled to **one free full credit report per bureau per calendar year**. Four bureaus means four free reports.

| Bureau | Where |
|---|---|
| **TransUnion CIBIL** | [cibil.com](https://www.cibil.com) |
| **Experian India** | [experian.in](https://www.experian.in) |
| **Equifax India** | [equifax.co.in](https://www.equifax.co.in) |
| **CRIF High Mark** | [crifhighmark.com](https://www.crifhighmark.com) |

Dispute errors with **both** the bureau and the lender that reported the entry. Closed loans still showing open, and accounts that are not yours, are the two most common defects.

**Escalation for a bank or card dispute:** bank's nodal grievance officer → 30 days → RBI Ombudsman via CMS.

## 18.9 Property

| What | Where |
|---|---|
| **RERA**: verify a project and developer before buying | Each state runs its own RERA portal; find yours via [rera.gov.in](https://rera.gov.in) |
| Stamp duty, registration, encumbrance certificates | State registration department portals; these are state-specific |
| Property tax | Municipal corporation portal for your city |
| TDS on property purchase above ₹50 lakh | Form 26QB, via the e-filing portal |

**Before any purchase:** check the RERA registration number, the encumbrance certificate, the approved plan, and the occupancy certificate. ## 18.10 Consumer protection

| What | Where |
|---|---|
| National Consumer Helpline | [consumerhelpline.gov.in](https://consumerhelpline.gov.in) (1915) |
| Cyber fraud, including financial fraud | [cybercrime.gov.in](https://cybercrime.gov.in) (1930) |

**If money leaves your account fraudulently, report it immediately.** RBI's limited-liability framework reduces or eliminates a customer's liability for unauthorised electronic transactions where the incident is reported promptly; the customer's exposure rises the longer the delay.

## 18.11 Things worth doing once

Each of these takes minutes, costs nothing, and is routinely left undone for years.

- [ ] **Activate your UAN** and seed Aadhaar, PAN and bank account: [member portal](https://unifiedportal-mem.epfindia.gov.in/memberinterface/)
- [ ] **File EPF e-nomination**: covers EPF, EPS and EDLI in one submission
- [ ] **Check your EPF passbook** and confirm whether your employer contributes on full Basic or on the ₹15,000 ceiling
- [ ] **Confirm old PF has transferred** rather than sitting dormant under a previous Member ID
- [ ] **Nominate** on every bank account, deposit, demat account, mutual fund folio, insurance policy, PPF account and NPS PRAN
- [ ] **Pull all four credit reports** and dispute any errors
- [ ] **Link PAN and Aadhaar**, and check your AIS against your own records
- [ ] **Verify your advisor's SEBI registration** if you use one, and establish who pays them
- [ ] **Ask HR** whether the company offers employer NPS under Section 124(2); it is the only significant deduction left in the new regime
- [ ] **Find the room-rent limit** on your employer's group health policy
- [ ] **Write a will**: a nominee is a trustee, not an heir
